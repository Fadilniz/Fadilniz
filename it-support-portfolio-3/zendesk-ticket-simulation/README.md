# Zendesk ticket simulation — Lab, practice data

- Free Zendesk trial, Oct 2026
- Requesters: practice test users only, no real customers, clients, or coworkers
- Completed Oct 24, 2026: 5 tickets marked Solved
- Method for every ticket: report, checks, fix or escalation, internal note, Solved

## Tickets resolved

### 1. POS terminal frozen mid-order — Incident, High, Solved
- Reported: terminal froze during dinner rush, orders could not be processed
- Checks: power cable secure, terminal restart
- Fix: terminal back online after restart, orders moved to second terminal while down

### 2. Receipt printer offline — Incident, High, Solved
- Reported: orders coming in, no receipts printing
- Checks: printer power, paper loaded, cable to POS secure, printer restart
- Fix: test print worked, orders printing again

### 3. Staff locked out of POS login — Incident, Medium, Solved
- Reported: staff member could not log in, could not take orders on that terminal
- Checks: username spelling, caps lock, account locked after failed attempts
- Fix: password reset, login tested, staff member able to log in again

### 4. Card reader failing — Incident, High, Solved
- Reported: reader losing connection during payment, customers could not pay by card
- Checks: reader connection and cable, reader restart, payment test
- Fix: reader working again, fallback to second terminal and escalation if failure repeated

### 5. Menu price wrong — Incident, Low, Solved
- Reported: price on POS did not match menu board, customers charged wrong price
- Checks: item name, correct price from menu board, manager approval for fix
- Fix: price corrected on POS and tested, matches menu board
